FAQ

What does Magy Production do?

Filming and video editing from Bucharest: social media reels, brand films and video for clinics, car showrooms, restaurants and events — concept, filming, editing and color grading.

Where do you shoot?

I'm based in Bucharest and shoot all over Romania and across Europe.

How much does a video cost?

It depends on how many videos you need, the shooting days and how complex the edit is. Fill in the contact form and you'll get a clear quote.

Do you work in English?

Yes — briefs, calls and on-set direction can all be in English.

Which formats do you deliver?

Vertical 9:16 for Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts and horizontal 16:9 for websites, YouTube or presentations — or both from the same shoot.

Can I send you just my footage to edit?

Yes. If you already have the footage, I'll edit and color grade it in DaVinci Resolve.
